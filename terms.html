Terms & Conditions

Use and privacy information for the RiverGuard educational prototype.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Purpose of this application

RiverGuard demonstrates how a trained machine-learning model can classify manually entered water measurements. Its dataset and labels are synthetic. Use the application for learning, demonstrations and exploratory comparisons.

2. Interpretation of results

Outputs such as “Lower concern” and “Needs verification” describe this demonstration model only. They do not certify water safety, potability or compliance. Model probabilities are uncalibrated. The application has no connected physical sensors and cannot confirm a pollution event. Real decisions require verified measurements and qualified review.

3. Your inputs and responsibility

Check units and values before interpreting an assessment. Avoid entering names, precise locations or other sensitive information in station labels, particularly on shared devices. Do not present synthetic outputs as measured environmental evidence.

4. Local storage and privacy

The application runs predictions in your browser and does not send sample measurements to an application server. When you select “Save sample,” it stores up to 50 samples in this browser’s local storage. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view them. “Clear saved history” removes the stored journal. Browser settings or clearing site data may also remove it. The site uses no application analytics or advertising trackers.

Your hosting provider may independently process normal website request information, such as IP addresses and access logs. This page describes the application’s behavior, not the hosting provider’s separate practices.

5. Downloads and persistence

CSV exports stay wherever you save them. Clearing browser history inside this app does not delete previously downloaded files. Local browser storage is not a backup. Export samples you want to retain, and keep downloaded files secure.

6. Availability and limitations

This student prototype may contain errors and may change. It does not provide an uptime or support commitment. A saved comparison shows numeric differences only and does not establish an environmental trend. Review the model evaluation and limitations before relying on an output.

7. Hosting and project ownership

The downloadable project can run locally or on a static host. No third-party hosting account, paid service or subscription is included. This page does not grant rights to third-party materials or replace any applicable software license.

8. Questions

For project questions, contact the person or institution that supplied your copy or the repository maintainer. This prototype does not provide an emergency reporting channel.
